How to Choose a Bridal Makeup Artist in Pretoria

Five things that separate a great bridal artist from a risky booking.

Pretoria brides are spoilt for choice — which makes choosing harder, not easier. After hundreds of weddings across Gauteng, Limpopo, Mpumalanga and the North West, here’s what we’d look for if we were booking.

1. A portfolio of real brides

Ask to see real clients, not stock or heavily-filtered images. Skin should still look like skin. A trustworthy bridal artist shows you exactly what you’ll get.

2. Range, not just a signature

Your artist should adapt to your features and style — soft and natural, or full glam — rather than painting every bride the same way.

3. A trial as standard

The trial is where trust is built and your look is perfected. If it isn’t offered, that’s a flag.

4. Transparent pricing

You should understand what you’re paying for before you commit — per person, by hair length, with travel made clear. Vague “from” ranges leave you guessing.

5. Calm, reliable communication

How an artist answers your first enquiry is how your wedding morning will feel. Choose the steady, kind, organised one.
