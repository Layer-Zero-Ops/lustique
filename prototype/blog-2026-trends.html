Bridal Beauty for 2026 & 2027: Soft, Timeless, You

Why the most-loved looks right now are the ones that still look like you in twenty years.

Trends come and go, but wedding photos are forever. The brides who love their pictures years later almost always chose looks that enhanced — rather than masked — who they are.

Skin that looks like skin

Luminous, lived-in skin beats heavy, flat coverage every time, especially in photographs. The prep matters as much as the product (see our skin-prep guide).

Soft definition

A softly-defined eye, a flush of warmth, a lip that’s a little more you — timeless, and it photographs beautifully from the first look to the last dance.

Hair that holds the moment

Romantic waves and elegant, secure updos that last through hugs, dancing and a Highveld afternoon.
