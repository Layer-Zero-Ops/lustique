Your privacy matters

Privacy & Cookie Policy

How we look after your information, in line with South Africa’s POPIA.

📋 Working draft. Please review with Zandeline (and ideally a legal professional) before this goes live. Figures are aligned to the 2026–2027 pricing.

Who we are

Lustique Co. (“we”, “us”) — bridal hair & makeup, 242 Frangipani Street, La Montagne, Pretoria. Contact: info@lustiqueco.co.za · 067 925 3894.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone, and the wedding details you share (date, venue/town, party, preferences) when you enquire or request a quote.
  • Basic website usage (via privacy-friendly analytics and cookies) to improve the site.
  • Marketing preferences, only if you opt in.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote, plan your booking, and provide our services.
  • To contact you about your enquiry or booking.
  • To send you helpful content or offers — only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To improve the website.

Lawful basis & consent (POPIA)

We process your information to respond to your enquiry and fulfil our services, and — for marketing — on the basis of your consent. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Sharing

We use trusted service providers (e.g. our email and scheduling tools) under confidentiality, only as needed to serve you. We do not sell your information. We may disclose information if legally required.

Security & retention

We use reputable, secured services and keep your information only as long as needed for your booking or as the law requires, then remove it.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or object to its processing. Email info@lustiqueco.co.za and we’ll help.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for the site to work and to understand how it’s used. You’ll be asked to accept or decline non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice anytime via the banner or your browser settings. Declining won’t stop you from using the site.

Updates

We may update this policy; the latest version always lives here.
