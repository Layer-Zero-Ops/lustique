Your Wedding-Morning Timeline

A calm, realistic getting-ready schedule for you and your whole party — so the morning feels unhurried.

Get your quote

The secret to a relaxed wedding morning is a realistic schedule. Here’s how we plan it — work backwards from the time you need to be photographed dressed and ready.

How long each look takes

  • Bride (hair + makeup): about 2.5 hours, scheduled last so you’re freshest.
  • Each additional person: roughly 45–60 minutes per service.
  • We bring additional artists for larger parties so several people are styled at once — your final schedule reflects this.

A sample morning (bride + 3, ceremony at 15:00)

TimeWhat’s happening
08:30Team arrives & sets up
09:00Bridesmaids & mom — hair & makeup begin (in rotation)
11:30Bride’s hair
12:30Bride’s makeup
13:30Touch-ups, lashes, final checks
14:00Into the dress · photographer ready

We send your exact schedule the week before, built around your venue, party size and photographer’s timing.

Make the morning lovely

  • Choose a room with good natural light and space to set up.
  • Have everyone’s hair clean and dry (unless we’ve advised otherwise).
  • Keep a light breakfast, water, and a button-up top handy (so nothing pulls over a finished look).
  • Build in 15 minutes of buffer — calm beats rushed, every time.
