Our Story

The artist a bride chooses
when she wants to feel most like herself.

Tailored hair and makeup for the bride who wants to feel like the most radiant version of herself.

Since 2018

More than makeup —
a feeling of calm.

At Lustique Co. we believe beauty is more than makeup and hairstyling — it’s about confidence, individuality, and a look that reflects you. What began with Zandeline in 2018 has grown into a team of nine bridal artists who bring over 40 years of combined experience to every wedding.

Our approach is personal and collaborative. From the moment you book your trial, we take the time to understand your style, your dress and your vision — then craft a look that feels effortlessly, unmistakably you.

We are a faith-led, family-spirited studio, and we count it a blessing to play a small part in one of the most important days of your life.
