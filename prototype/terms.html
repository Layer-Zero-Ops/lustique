Service Agreement & Terms

Everything we agree on, in plain language — so your day is calm and there are no surprises.

📋 Working draft. Please review with Zandeline (and ideally a legal professional) before this goes live. Figures are aligned to the 2026–2027 pricing.

1. Booking & securing your date

A 50% booking fee secures your date and is required to confirm your booking. If that isn’t comfortable upfront, we offer a split: 25% now to hold the date, and the remaining 25% on a date we agree, or together with the balance. Until the booking fee is received, your date remains open to other enquiries.

The balance is due no later than 14 working days before your wedding, by EFT. If the balance isn’t received in time, we may treat the booking as cancelled.

2. The trial

Your trial is the heart of the experience — a relaxed, by-appointment session at one of our studios (we don’t travel for trials). We’ll shape your look together; bring inspiration images, any hair pieces or your veil, and come with clean, moisturised skin. Trials are usually held a few weeks to a month before the wedding.

3. Travel

On your wedding day, we come to you. Travel is free within 30 km of our La Montagne studio (242 Frangipani Street, Pretoria), then R7 per km (round trip) beyond that. Weddings more than 200 km one way are destination weddings, quoted individually; where an overnight stay is required, the client provides reasonable accommodation (minimum 3-star) or an agreed accommodation fee.

4. Your bridal party & additional artists

Makeup and hair are priced per person and by hair length. For larger parties, one or more additional artists join so everyone is ready, calm, and on time. Once a package including an assistant is booked, the party size can’t be reduced below that assistant threshold (the assistant booking is non-refundable).

5. On the day

We’ll send a detailed time schedule the week before. Please allow the time indicated for each service. Overtime caused by client-side delays is charged at R250 per 30 minutes. Please let us know in advance if no food or refreshments will be available for the team, so we can plan.

6. Changes, postponements & cancellations

Postponement: one free postponement to a weekday is allowed; a weekend postponement carries a R1 000 fee, and each further postponement R1 000, subject to availability on the new date.

Cancellation by you: the booking fee is non-refundable. Cancellations closer to the date may incur additional charges on a sliding scale, as set out in your signed agreement, to cover the reserved time and any artists already committed.

Cancellation by us (rare — illness or emergency): we’ll do everything to arrange a qualified replacement from our team; if we can’t, you receive a full refund of amounts paid.

7. Skin, allergies & comfort

Please disclose any allergies, sensitivities or skin conditions when you enquire and again at your trial, so we can patch-test and choose suitable products. Your comfort and safety come first.

8. Photography & your images

We love sharing our work. We only use images of you publicly with your permission, and we credit the photographer. You can ask us not to use your images at any time.

9. Promotions

Standing offers (such as “book 4+ and the least-expensive look is free”) apply as described at the time of booking and can’t be combined unless stated. Component pricing applies unless a promotion is running.

Questions about anything here? Email info@lustiqueco.co.za — we’re happy to talk it through.
